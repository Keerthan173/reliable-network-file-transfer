India is the seventh-largest country by land area and a vibrant global democracy.

Introduction
India, officially known as Bharat, is located in South Asia.
It has a population of over 1.4 billion people. The capital city is New Delhi.
India gained independence on August 15, 1947, after a long freedom struggle led by leaders like Mahatma Gandhi and Jawaharlal Nehru.
Geography and SymbolsLandscapes: The snowy Himalayas guard the north, the Thar Desert lies in the west, and tropical beaches line the southern coast.
Major rivers like the Ganges support farming.National Symbols: The national flag is a tricolor of saffron, white, and green.
The tiger is the national animal, the peacock is the national bird, and the lotus is the national flower.

Culture and Heritage
India is famous for the phrase "unity in diversity". People of many different religions, languages, and traditions live together in peace.

Languages:
The country recognizes 22 main languages, with hundreds of local dialects.

Festivals:
Major celebrations include Diwali, Holi, Eid, and Christmas.

Monuments: World-famous landmarks like the Taj Mahal showcase the country's rich history.
Economy and Modern GrowthIndia has a fast-growing economy driven by technology, agriculture, and services.
Cities like Bangalore serve as major global IT hubs.
While challenges like poverty and infrastructure development remain, the nation continues to make great strides in science, education, and space exploration.
